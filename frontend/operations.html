Configure the Person In Charge (PIC) for each site. WhatsApp check-in and check-out alerts are routed here. If a PIC resigns, simply click Edit / Replace to update their name and WhatsApp number.

💡 Note for Newly Registered PICs:
Because WhatsApp uses end-to-end encryption, when you register or replace a PIC number, have that phone tap "💬 Send Hi to Bot" once (or message "Hi" to +60 17-531 4868). This establishes the encryption keys so attendance alerts and ringtones arrive immediately without showing "Waiting for this message".
